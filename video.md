# Settle — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

**Format:** screen recording + voiceover. **Tone:** professional, conversational, confident — not hyped, not a monotone read. Talk like you're showing a colleague something you're proud of, not reciting a pitch deck.

**Total runtime target: 3:00.** Timestamps are cue points, not hard cuts — pace naturally and let a beat run a few seconds long or short if it feels right; just land the close by 3:00.

---

### [0:00–0:12] Cold open — the problem

**ON SCREEN:** Quick cuts of a few chain logos / wallet balances scattered across different networks, then cut to the Settle landing page hero.

**SAY:**
> "Your money isn't on one chain anymore. Some of it's on Arbitrum, some on Base, maybe some on Polygon. So why should a buy-now-pay-later decision only ever look at one of them?"

---

### [0:12–0:28] What Settle is

**ON SCREEN:** Settle logo animation, tagline on screen.

**SAY:**
> "This is Settle — buy now pay later, subscriptions, and recurring investing, built on Arbitrum, powered by Particle Network's Universal Accounts and Magic's email login. No seed phrase. No bridging. Your balance, wherever it actually lives, just works."

---

### [0:28–0:40] Onboarding

**ON SCREEN:** Magic email OTP flow — type an email, enter the code, land on the dashboard.

**SAY:**
> "Getting in takes ten seconds. Type your email, enter the code that lands in your inbox, and you've got a wallet — no extension, no seed phrase to lose."

---

### [0:40–1:15] Checkout — BNPL with the down-payment cap

**ON SCREEN:** Browse the catalog → open an item → hit "Buy Now" → down-payment screen appears → confirm → charge created.

**SAY:**
> "Let's buy something. I'll pick an item and hit Buy Now. Behind the scenes, a five-signal underwriter scores me in real time — wallet age, repayment history, even my balance across eight chains. If I'm approved, Settle doesn't front the whole purchase — it finances a slice, ten to thirty percent, scaled to my score. I pay the rest upfront, directly to the merchant, verified on-chain before my charge is even created."

---

### [1:15–1:45] Credit profile

**ON SCREEN:** Profile page → connect an exchange account or GitHub → score recomputes → explicit before/after message appears.

**SAY:**
> "Want a bigger limit? Connect an exchange account or your GitHub — read-only, nothing risky. Settle pulls in your trading history or your dev reputation, and the moment you connect, your score recomputes right there. You see exactly what changed, not a mystery number."

---

### [1:45–2:15] Pay Now — cross-chain repayment

**ON SCREEN:** Dashboard → click "Pay Now" on a due charge → brief loading state → confirmed.

**SAY:**
> "When an installment's due, I just hit Pay Now. Settle's Universal Account sources the USDC from whatever chain I actually hold it on, and settles it on Arbitrum. I never touched a bridge. I never picked a network."

---

### [2:15–2:40] Subscriptions + DCA

**ON SCREEN:** Quick cuts — subscribe to a plan; create a DCA plan (pick an asset, an amount, a schedule).

**SAY:**
> "The same engine powers subscriptions — cancel anytime, and your dashboard always shows exactly where you stand. And recurring DCA lets you auto-invest into any coin you hold, on any chain, on autopilot."

---

### [2:40–2:55] Merchant side

**ON SCREEN:** Merchant onboarding wizard — quick click-through of the steps.

**SAY:**
> "Merchants onboard just as fast — configure a payout wallet on-chain, list a product, and start accepting BNPL and subscriptions in minutes."

---

### [2:55–3:00] Close

**ON SCREEN:** Settle logo + URL, hold for the last two seconds.

**SAY:**
> "Settle. Payments that follow your money — not the other way around."

---

## Production notes

- Record the walkthrough beats (checkout, credit profile, Pay Now, subscriptions/DCA, merchant) against real testnet-cents-priced catalog items so nothing looks staged or expensive.
- Keep cursor movement deliberate and slow down right before each click so viewers can follow the UI, not just the narration.
- If a beat runs long in practice, trim the merchant-side segment first (2:40–2:55) — it's the least essential to the core BNPL/credit story.
